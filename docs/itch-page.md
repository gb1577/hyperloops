# itch.io page

## Upload settings

- **Kind of project:** HTML
- **Upload:** `release/hyperloops-itch.zip` (made from `final/index.html` after `npm run build`), tick **This file will be played in the browser**
- **Embed:** 1280 × 720, **Fullscreen button** on (or **Click to launch in fullscreen**). Tick **Mobile friendly**, landscape, if you want phones
- **Trailer / screenshots:** `video/hyperloops-teaser.mp4` (16:9) and `video/hyperloops-teaser-vertical.mp4` (9:16, for social posts). Upload the video to YouTube, since itch only takes trailer links
- **Genre:** Rhythm · **Tags:** rhythm, runner, low-poly, music, arcade, webgpu, three.js

## Page text

**Short description:** Fly a shard of light through low-poly worlds, on the beat.

---

**HYPERLOOPS** is a rhythm graze-runner. Crystal spikes erupt on the beat, and you thread your light-shard
through them, as close as you dare. Brush past for points, grab the gold shards hidden in the gaps, then dive
through the hyperloop at the end of each level into the next world.

**15 levels** · original **160 BPM** soundtrack · every spike lands on the music

- **Graze** spikes without touching them to build your multiplier
- **Collect** gold shards for a rising chain of chimes
- **Skim** low over the ground for bonus score
- **Boost** for double score: the ship surges ahead and the view widens
- **Easy** or **Hard**: more shields and fewer spikes, or the full pattern

### Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| Steer | mouse · WASD / arrows · gamepad · touch-drag |
| Boost | hold Space / left click |
| Pause · Mute | Esc · M |

Runs in the browser with WebGPU and falls back to WebGL2. Best with headphones.

*Made from my 2016 After Effects music video* Hyperloops. *The first ten levels recreate its scenes.*
